Seam Math

The pattern piece is the finished size. The fabric needs the rest.

Boundary: arithmetic only Seam Math adds and scales the numbers you type, in your own unit, rounded to hundredths. It does not say what seam allowance or hem a pattern should use, how a garment will fit, or how fabric will behave. Your pattern and your fabric decide that.

The cut truth

Finished 10 x 8 with a 0.5 seam allowance on every edge: cut 11 x 9. The allowance goes on both sides.

The hem truth

Finished length 30, hem depth 1.5, turned twice: cut 33. A hem turned twice takes the depth twice.

The scale truth

Test square should measure 4, measured 3.8: set the print scale to 105.3%.

Run your numbers
Seam Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter, nothing more.
